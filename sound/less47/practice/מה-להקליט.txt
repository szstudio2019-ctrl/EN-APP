שיעור 47 – סיפורים מהנים · תרגול (משבצות צהובות במסמך)
כל מילה/צליל בקובץ נפרד בשם שלו; משפטים וסיפורים ממוספרים.

story-01.mp3 – A big cat sat at the sea. The cat ate meat and felt happy. A man with a bag met the cat. The man had fish in his bag. The cat sat and ate the fish. It was a good day.
story-02.mp3 – This is Tim. Tim is a kid. Tim has a big hat. His hat is a gift. Tim has a map. Tim wants a big trip. He will go up the hill. Tim ran and ran. He did not sit still.
story-03.mp3 – Dan likes to swim at the pool. He can see the moon in the cool water. Dan took a book to read. He sat on the wood bench. The food was good. Dan ate a big meal and felt full.
story-04.mp3 – The snail will stay on the globe. The snail has a green home on its back. It moves slow but it is safe. At night the snail sleeps near the stone. The moon is bright. The snail is not afraid.
story-05.mp3 – Jake found a bone and a dime in the mine. He rode his bike to the lake to clean them. Jake put his notebook on the white desk and wrote about his day. He hopes to find more things in the mine next time.
story-06.mp3 – The knight rode through the dark night. The moonlight lit his path. He had a bright flashlight too. At midnight he saw a big castle. The light inside was warm. A kind mother opened the door. She gave him food and a place to sleep. It was the highlight of his long flight.
